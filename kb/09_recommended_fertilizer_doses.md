# Recommended Fertilizer Doses

Source: TNAU Agritech portal (Tamil Nadu Agricultural University), retrieved 12 November 2026; ragi uses the Karnataka recommendation on that portal; potato: UAS Bangalore. Soil test rule: ICAR, Indian Farming. These are general recommended doses per hectare (one hectare is about 2.5 acres). A Soil Health Card or the local agriculture officer may recommend a different dose for your field, and that recommendation should be followed.

## Adjusting the dose with a soil test
Soil test results group each nutrient as low, medium or high. The general recommended dose is for a medium soil. When the soil test shows a nutrient is low, the dose of that nutrient is increased by 25 percent, and when it is high, the dose is reduced by 25 percent. The Smart Krishi screen applies this rule when the farmer enters the Soil Health Card ratings.

## Tomato fertilizer dose
For tomato varieties, the basal dose is farmyard manure 25 tonnes per hectare, nitrogen 75 kg, phosphorus (P2O5) 100 kg and potassium (K2O) 50 kg per hectare, with borax 10 kg and zinc sulphate 50 kg per hectare. A top dressing of 75 kg nitrogen per hectare is given on the 30th day after planting or during earthing up. The total for the season is 150 kg nitrogen, 100 kg phosphorus and 50 kg potassium per hectare. Hybrids have a different recommendation.

## Paddy fertilizer dose
For medium and long duration transplanted rice, the blanket recommendation is 150 kg nitrogen, 50 kg phosphorus (P2O5) and 50 kg potassium (K2O) per hectare. Nitrogen and potassium are applied in four equal splits, at basal, tillering, panicle initiation and heading stages, and all the phosphorus is applied as basal. Tillering and panicle initiation are crucial stages, so the quantity should not be reduced there.

## Ragi fertilizer dose
For irrigated ragi in Karnataka, the recommendation is 100 kg nitrogen, 50 kg phosphorus (P2O5) and 50 kg potassium (K2O) per hectare, with farmyard manure 7 to 12 tonnes per hectare. Half of the nitrogen and all the phosphorus and potassium are applied at sowing, and the remaining nitrogen is given in two equal splits after 30 and 50 days of sowing.

## Maize fertilizer dose
For hybrid maize, the recommendation is 150 kg nitrogen, 75 kg phosphorus (P2O5) and 75 kg potassium (K2O) per hectare. A quarter of the nitrogen and all the phosphorus and potassium are applied at sowing, half of the nitrogen at 25 days after sowing, and the last quarter at 45 days after sowing.

## Onion fertilizer dose
For Bellary onion, the basal dose is farmyard manure 25 tonnes per hectare, Azospirillum 2 kg and Phosphobacteria 2 kg per hectare, with nitrogen 50 kg, phosphorus 150 kg and potassium 75 kg per hectare. A top dressing of 50 kg nitrogen per hectare is given at 30 days after planting. Zinc sulphate 50 kg per hectare is applied at the last ploughing.

## Potato fertilizer dose
For potato in Karnataka, the recommended dose used by the University of Agricultural Sciences, Bangalore at Hassan is farmyard manure 25 tonnes per hectare with 75 kg nitrogen, 75 kg phosphorus (P2O5) and 100 kg potassium (K2O) per hectare. Nitrogen is given in two splits, half at planting and half at earthing up, and all the phosphorus and potassium at planting. Source: Mysore Journal of Agricultural Sciences (UAS Bangalore), 2020.

## Coffee manuring
For coffee, fertilizer is applied in several splits through the year, at pre-blossom, post-blossom or pre-monsoon, mid-monsoon and post-monsoon, and the total dose depends on the crop's age and expected yield. A single split should not be more than 40 kg nitrogen, 30 kg phosphorus and 40 kg potassium per hectare, because a bigger single dose can scorch the plants. Bulky organic manure such as farmyard manure or compost is applied at 5 tonnes per hectare once in two years. For the exact dose for your plantation, ask the Coffee Board's extension office, because it depends on yield, variety and soil test.

## Converting nutrients into fertilizer bags
Urea contains 46 percent nitrogen. DAP contains 18 percent nitrogen and 46 percent phosphorus (P2O5). Muriate of potash (MOP) contains 60 percent potassium (K2O). The phosphorus is usually given as DAP, whose nitrogen counts towards the nitrogen dose, and the remaining nitrogen is given as urea. The Smart Krishi screen does this calculation for the farmer's crop and area.
