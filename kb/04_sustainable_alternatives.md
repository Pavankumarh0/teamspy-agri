# Sustainable Alternatives to Chemical Fertilizers

Review status: starter content, to be checked against the official package of practices.

## Compost and farmyard manure
Well-decomposed compost and farmyard manure add organic matter and a range of nutrients, improve soil structure and water holding, and feed soil life. Use only well-decomposed manure, because fresh manure can burn roots, carry weed seeds and take nitrogen away from the crop while it breaks down.

## Vermicompost
Vermicompost is compost made by earthworms. It is rich in nutrients in a form plants can use easily and it improves soil structure. It can be made on the farm from crop waste and cow dung.

## Green manure
Green manure crops such as sunhemp and dhaincha are grown and then ploughed into the soil before they flower. They add organic matter, and because they are legumes they also add nitrogen to the soil.

## Biofertilizers
Biofertilizers contain helpful microbes. Rhizobium helps legume crops fix nitrogen from the air. Azotobacter and Azospirillum help supply nitrogen to non-legume crops. Phosphate solubilising bacteria make phosphorus in the soil available to plants. They are usually applied as a seed treatment, seedling dip or mixed with manure, following the instructions on the packet, and they work best in soil with enough organic matter.

## Neem cake
Neem cake is the residue left after pressing neem seeds. It adds organic matter and some nutrients, and it is used against some soil pests such as nematodes.

## Crop rotation and intercropping
Rotating cereals and vegetables with legume crops such as pulses adds nitrogen and breaks pest and disease cycles. Intercropping a legume with a main crop also improves soil fertility.

## Combining organic and chemical sources
Organic sources release nutrients slowly and may not meet the full need of a high-yielding crop on their own. Integrated nutrient management, which combines organic manures and biofertilizers with a reduced, soil-test-based amount of chemical fertilizer, keeps yields up while protecting soil health.
