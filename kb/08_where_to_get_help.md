# Where to Get Agricultural Help

Review status: starter content, to be checked against the official package of practices.

## Local agricultural support
Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs) are farm science centres in each district that give farmers advice, training and demonstrations. In Karnataka, Raitha Samparka Kendras at the hobli level give advice and supply inputs such as seeds and biofertilizers. The local agriculture officer and the state agricultural university can also advise on crop varieties, fertilizer doses and pest problems.

## Soil Health Card
Under the Soil Health Card scheme, farmers can get their soil tested and receive a card that shows the nutrient status of their field and recommends fertilizer for their crops. Contact your local agriculture office or Raitha Samparka Kendra to have your soil sampled.

## Pesticide safety
Use a pesticide only when a pest or disease is confirmed, choose a product recommended for that problem by agricultural experts, and follow the label for the dose and the waiting period before harvest. Wear protective clothing while spraying and keep pesticides away from children, food and water sources.
